# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

iReborn does not collect, sell, or share personal data, and it does not track you. The only information that ever reaches the developer is a problem report or feedback that you choose to send.

## Data Collection

iReborn does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, location, or tracking data. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Local Data

The apps you import, their saved games, your library, and your settings stay on your device. The app does not send this information to the developer or to any analytics service.

## Problem Reports and Feedback

When an app fails, or when you choose Report a Problem or Send Feedback, iReborn shows you what will be sent before you send it. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

A report can include:

- The app's name, bundle identifier, version, and a fingerprint (hash) of its file, used to tell app versions apart
- A technical description of what went wrong, and the last part of the app's own log output
- Anything you write in the report
- The iReborn version, your device model (for example, "iPhone18,1"), and its iOS version

Reports never include your name, your Apple Account, your device's name, or your saved games. They are stored in iReborn's CloudKit database on Apple's iCloud servers and are used only to improve compatibility. CloudKit attaches an anonymous, app-specific identifier to each report; it does not reveal who you are.

If you are offline or not signed in to iCloud, a report waits on your device until it can be sent, or you can share it yourself as a file.

## iCloud Sync

If you turn on iCloud saves, your saved games and settings are stored in your own iCloud account so they can sync between your devices. This data goes to Apple's iCloud, not to the developer, and the developer cannot access it. Imported app files are never synced.

## Permissions

The apps you open in iReborn may ask to use the camera, microphone, photo library, location, motion sensors, music library, or local network, as they did on the original iPhone. iReborn asks for your permission first and passes the result only to the app that asked. None of it is sent to the developer.

## Network Access

Imported apps start offline. If you turn on network access for an app, that app can connect to its own online services, which have their own privacy practices.

## Purchases

iReborn Pro is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer does not receive your payment information.

## Crash Reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

## Tracking

iReborn does not track users across apps, websites, or services.

## Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, share, or disclose report data to anyone.

## Children’s Privacy

iReborn does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated if the app’s privacy practices change. Any updates will be posted in this repository.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the public GitHub repository.
